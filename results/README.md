# Results

This folder presents the major findings, statistical outputs, and spatial visualisations of the study on microclimatic variations across Jahangirnagar University campus.

### Key findings

- **LULC:** The campus contains a diverse landscape of forested areas, built-up areas, open fields, and water bodies.
- **Temperature:** ERA5 data show seasonal and monthly variations in air temperature, with the highest monthly average maximum temperature occurring in May.
- **Land Surface Temperature:** Spatial LST ranges from 24.28°C to 31.99°C, indicating notable thermal differences across the campus.
- **LST trends:** Annual average maximum LST shows interannual variability during 2011–2025, but the overall trend is not statistically significant.
- **NDVI:** Vegetation density varies across the campus, reflecting differences in vegetation cover.
- **Microclimatic zones:** Four zones were identified: cool vegetation, moderate mixed, warm built, and heat-intensive zones.
